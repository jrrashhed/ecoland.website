Premium Fresh Egyptian Onions for Global Markets

Ecoland is a professional Egyptian grower and exporter of premium fresh onions, supplying high-quality produce tailored to the requirements of international wholesale and retail markets. Our onions are cultivated in Egypt’s fertile agricultural regions, benefiting from rich soil composition and optimal climate conditions that ensure uniform bulb size, strong skin quality, and excellent pungency. Harvested at peak maturity and properly cured, Ecoland onions are ideal for long-distance export, offering outstanding firmness, freshness, and extended shelf life.
