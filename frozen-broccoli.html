Frequently Asked Questions

Is frozen broccoli ready to cook directly?

Yes, cook it frozen in boiling water for 3-5 minutes or according to your recipe.

Does frozen broccoli retain its nutritional value?

Yes, quick freezing preserves vitamins and minerals up to 95%.

What's the difference between whole and cut florets?

Whole florets are suitable for premium presentation, while cut ones are ideal for quick cooking and mixes.

Can frozen broccoli be used in salads?

Yes, after cooking and cooling it can be added to cold and hot salads.

What is the shelf life?

Up to 24 months when stored at -18°C or below.

Why is broccoli considered a superfood?

Because it contains exceptional amounts of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants.
