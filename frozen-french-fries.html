Frequently Asked Questions

Are the French fries ready to cook directly?

Yes, they are pre-blanched and par-fried. Simply fry frozen in hot oil or bake in the oven.

What is the shelf life?

Up to 24 months when stored at -18°C or below.

Is the product Halal certified?

Yes, all our products are HALAL certified.

What packaging sizes are available?

We offer 1 kg, 2.5 kg, 10 kg, and 20 kg bags according to customer requirements.

Do you export to all markets?

Yes, we export to Europe, the Gulf, Africa, and Asia in compliance with international standards.
