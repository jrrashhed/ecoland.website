Premium IQF Frozen Egyptian Strawberries

Ecoland is a professional Egyptian processor and exporter of premium IQF frozen strawberries, supplying high-quality frozen fruit to international food service, industrial, and retail markets. Our strawberries are sourced from selected farms, processed at peak ripeness, and individually quick frozen to preserve natural color, taste, and nutritional value.
