A balanced blend of the finest fresh Egyptian vegetables, offering convenience while preserving nutritional value and natural taste. IQF frozen to ensure each vegetable retains its unique quality. Frequently Asked Questions

Are frozen mixed vegetables ready to cook?

Yes, add directly to cooking without thawing.

What is the cooking time?

5–7 minutes boiling or 8–10 minutes stir-fry depending on recipe.

Can I request a custom mix?

Yes, we provide customized blends based on client needs.

Do vegetables retain nutritional value?

Each vegetable is frozen separately before mixing to preserve nutrients.

What cooking methods are recommended?

Boiling, steaming, stir-frying, or oven cooking.

What is the difference between mixes?

Each mix is designed for specific cuisines and uses.

Is this product suitable for restaurants?

Yes, it ensures consistent quality and cost efficiency.
