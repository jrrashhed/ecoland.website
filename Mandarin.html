Ecoland is a professional Egyptian grower and exporter of premium fresh mandarins (easy peelers), supplying high-quality citrus tailored to international wholesale and retail markets. Our mandarins are grown in Egypt’s prime citrus regions, known for their vibrant color, easy peeling, balanced sweetness, and excellent shelf life.
