Our Story

Global Impact

Our Farm-to-Port Products Supply Chain

Professional handling ensures our products arrive fresh and firm.

🌱

Farming & Cultivation

Cultivated in optimal Egyptian soil conditions with managed irrigation to ensure uniform tuber size and skin quality.

🤲

Harvesting & Collection

Harvested carefully to minimize skin abrasions and mechanical damage, then transported immediately to shaded collection centers.

🔍

Quality Control & Sorting

Potatoes undergo a thorough dry sorting process, followed by strict size calibration and defect removal to meet export grades.

🏭

Packaging & Shipping

Packed in customer-specified PP, Jute, or Jumbo bags. Loaded into 40ft reefer containers with precise ventilation settings for transit.
