About Us


ECOLAND was founded with a vision to bring Egypt’s finest agricultural products to the world.

From sustainable farming to certified export standards, our journey is rooted in quality, trust, and global impact.

Through strategic agricultural investments and modern cultivation practices, we ensure consistent supply, strict quality control, and full traceability at every stage of production.

Our operations are guided by internationally recognized certifications, enabling us to meet the demands of global markets while building long-term partnerships based on reliability, transparency, and excellenc.
