Premium Fresh Egyptian Potatoes for Global Markets

Ecoland is a professional Egyptian grower and exporter of premium fresh potatoes, supplying high-quality produce tailored to the requirements of international wholesale and retail markets. Our potatoes are cultivated in Egypt’s fertile agricultural regions, benefiting from rich soil composition and optimal climate conditions that ensure consistent size, skin quality, and taste. Harvested at peak maturity and carefully handled and graded, Ecoland potatoes are well suited for long-distance export, offering excellent firmness, freshness, and extended shelf life for global distribution.
