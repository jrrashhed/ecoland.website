Premium Egyptian Alfalfa Hay for Global Animal Feed Markets

Ecoland is a leading Egyptian producer and exporter of premium quality alfalfa hay (Medicago sativa), also known as Lucerne or Berseem Hegazey. Our alfalfa is cultivated in Egypt's fertile Nile Delta and New Valley regions, where abundant sunshine, controlled irrigation, and rich soil create ideal conditions for producing high-protein, nutrient-dense forage. Harvested at optimal maturity stages and processed using advanced baling and dehydration techniques, Ecoland alfalfa hay delivers superior nutritional value, excellent digestibility, and consistent quality that meets the demanding requirements of dairy farms, equestrian facilities, and livestock operations worldwide.

Our Farm-to-Port Export Process

Maintaining premium nutritional quality from harvest to global delivery.

🌱

Cultivation & Growing

Grown in carefully selected agricultural areas with optimal irrigation and soil conditions. Multiple cutting cycles ensure year-round availability and consistent nutritional profiles.

✋

Harvesting at Peak Nutrition

Harvested at the pre-bloom to early bloom stage to maximize protein content and digestibility. Timing is critical to ensure optimal nutrient retention and palatability.

🔥

Drying & Processing

Sun-dried or dehydrated to reduce moisture content below 15%, preventing mold and preserving nutritional value. Processed through advanced cleaning and grading systems.

📦

Baling & Packaging

Compressed into small or large bales, double-compressed bales, pellets, or cubes based on customer requirements. Quality testing ensures compliance with international feed standards.

🚢

Export & Logistics

Carefully loaded in containers to maximize capacity while preventing damage. Shipped with full phytosanitary documentation to meet importing country requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

We offer flexible programs starting from one 40ft container (approximately 20-26 tons depending on compression level).

What animals is Egyptian alfalfa suitable for?

Our premium alfalfa is ideal for dairy cattle, beef cattle, horses, sheep, goats, camels, and rabbits. Different grades are available for different nutritional requirements.

How do you ensure consistent quality?

We implement strict quality control at every stage: optimal harvest timing, controlled drying processes, laboratory testing for protein and moisture content, and careful handling to minimize leaf loss.

What makes Egyptian alfalfa special?

Egypt's climate allows for 6-8 cutting cycles per year, ensuring consistent availability. Our abundant sunshine and controlled irrigation produce alfalfa with excellent protein content, vibrant green color, and superior digestibility.

What packaging formats are available?

We offer small bales (20-25kg), large bales (300-500kg), double-compressed bales for maximum container efficiency, pellets, cubes, and custom packaging solutions based on your requirements.
